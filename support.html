Tell Me support

Tell Me is a voice helper. Tap the red button and speak. The phone answers out loud.

How to use it

  1. On the first screen, choose a language: English, Hindi, Telugu, Kannada, Tamil, or Malayalam.
  2. You can save one family name and phone number, or leave them blank.
  3. Tap the red button and speak. A countdown shows the time left. Recording stops at 0, after about a minute. Tap again to stop sooner.
  4. Listen to the reply. If Tell Me needs one detail, it asks. It asks at most three follow-up questions, then asks you to try again from the start.
  5. The small gear icon changes the language, the family number, and can call the saved family number.

What you can ask

  • A place or directions. Tell Me opens Google Maps.
  • A phone call. Tell Me opens the dialer. You still tap call.
  • A WhatsApp message. Tell Me opens WhatsApp.
  • A payment. Tell Me opens your UPI app or explains the steps. It never asks for your PIN.
  • A train, a PNR, or IRCTC. Tell Me speaks the steps and opens the official IRCTC site. It does not book the ticket.
  • A short everyday question.

If someone asks you to share an OTP, a KYC code, a lottery prize, or an unknown link, Tell Me tells you to stop and does not open that link.

What Tell Me cannot do

  • It cannot see or enter a UPI PIN.
  • It cannot sign in to IRCTC or buy a ticket for you.
  • It is not a bank, a doctor, or a government office.
  • It has no account to sign in to, and there is no Tell Me website login.

If the microphone is blocked, allow it in the phone’s settings, then tap the red button again. If a language is not recognized, download that speech language in the phone’s speech settings. That download is part of the phone, not a Tell Me charge.
